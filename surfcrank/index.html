Significant wave height is the average of the largest third of waves. It's measured at the buoy, in about 135 ft of water, so it won't match the size of the waves breaking at the beach. Times are Pacific.

Data from the Coastal Data Information Program (CDIP), Scripps Institution of Oceanography, station 201p1.
